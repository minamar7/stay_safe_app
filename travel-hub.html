⚠️ Scam Alerts
Paris, France

Watch out for the "Friendship Bracelet" scam at Sacré-Cœur and pickpockets near the Eiffel Tower.

Rome, Italy

"Free" roses or pictures with Gladiators usually lead to aggressive demands for money.

Bangkok, Thailand

The "Grand Palace is closed" scam. Tuk-tuk drivers will try to take you to overpriced jewelry shops instead.

Barcelona, Spain

Bird poop scam: Someone sprays a liquid on you, offers to help clean it, and robs you during the process.
